03 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The introductory lesson starts with concepts. Coding can become useful later, depending on the career direction you choose.

Are all six modules available?

The foundations lesson and quiz are available now. The remaining roadmap modules describe the planned curriculum; they are not published courses yet.

Will I receive a certificate?

This starter site does not issue certificates, provide accreditation or promise employment. It is an introduction to the subject.

Does this site save my progress?

No. The eight-question quiz runs in your browser, gives explanations and topic feedback, and resets when the page reloads. This version has no accounts, tracking scripts or registration form.
